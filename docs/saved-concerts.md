# Saved shows and archive

Open **My shows** to see saved concerts. Each show has four personal statuses:
**Interested**, **Going**, **Went**, and **Didn't go**. Saving a show starts it as
Interested. Change its status on the saved card or concert details, and use
**Filter by status** to narrow the list.

**Archive** shows past saved concerts and, when signed in, all your past private
workbook imports, including imports you never bookmarked. It lists the most
recent dates first. A show belongs here only after its whole date span has
ended; today's shows, ongoing festivals, and unconfirmed dates are not archived.
The browser's local day is the reference.

Passing a date never changes Going to Went. Unbookmarked imports show **Set
status** until you choose one; choosing a status also saves that concert.
Original workbook attendance fields, notes, and wording stay unchanged.
Removing a bookmark removes its personal status, not the underlying imported
record, so a past private import still appears in Archive.

Account statuses sync with your account and are included in its private export.
Guest saves and statuses stay in this browser, survive reloads, and are not
silently uploaded on sign-in. Guest lists filter and sort all available saved
shows before pagination. Missing older public listings cannot be displayed.
Blocked storage or failed updates show an error and keep the last saved status.
Keyboard focus returns to the status control for retries, or to the status
filter when a changed concert no longer matches it.

Personal statuses never change the public concert's scheduled/cancelled status
and are not published in feeds, calendar links, analytics, or provider requests.
